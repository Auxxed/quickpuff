# Source recordings

Field recordings that `tools/make-sounds.py` builds some cues on. Every one is
from [Freesound](https://freesound.org) under **Creative Commons 0** (public
domain): no conditions on copying, changing or redistributing it, commercial
use included. Each was checked on its Freesound page. Credit is not required,
but here it is anyway.

The clips are Freesound's high-quality previews, trimmed (start and length
below) and re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis at 48 kHz.
